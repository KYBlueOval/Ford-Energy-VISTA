# VISTA 2.6.1 — Executive Governance & Responsibility UI

## Included

- Rebuilt Administration Sponsor Executive Oversight as a branded governance workspace.
- Added a direct Administration link to the all-Sponsor visitor workspace.
- Plant Leaders and Administrators can review all Sponsor accounts, Sponsor performance, sponsored visitors, delegated coverage, and operational responsibility.
- Plant Leader access remains read-only for visitor decisions and responsibility changes.
- Primary Sponsors and Administrators can assign or change the operationally responsible person from an individual visitor record.
- Visitor rows display the primary Sponsor and current operational responsibility.
- Rebuilt the EV Charging decision dialog with aligned summary fields, a full-width decision area, responsive layout, and a clear notification workflow.

## Deployment

1. Push the complete repository to GitHub Pages.
2. Replace the Apps Script project `Code.gs` with `apps-script/Code.gs`.
3. Save and create a new web-app deployment version while preserving the existing URL.
4. No `setupVista()` run is required when upgrading from 2.6.0.
5. Hard-refresh Administration and the Sponsor Portal with `Ctrl + F5`.

## Prove-out

1. Sign in to Administration and open **Sponsor Approval Governance**.
2. Confirm the new executive workspace, workflow guidance, assignment cards, and **Open All Sponsor Visitors** action.
3. Sign in to the Sponsor Portal as an Administrator and confirm all visitors display with Sponsor and responsibility labels.
4. Open a visitor, select **Assign Responsible Person**, choose an active salaried Sponsor/Approver/Plant Leader account, enter a reason, and save.
5. Confirm the visitor row and detail card display the assignee and the Audit Log records `VISIT_OPERATIONAL_SPONSOR_ASSIGNED`.
6. Sign in as a Plant Leader and confirm all Sponsors, performance standings, visitors, and coverage are visible while approval/edit/delegation controls remain hidden.
7. In Administration open **EV Charging Access Control**, approve or deny a request, and confirm the revised decision dialog is aligned and responsive.
