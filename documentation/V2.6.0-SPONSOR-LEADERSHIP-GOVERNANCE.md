# VISTA 2.6.0 — Sponsor Leadership, Responsibility & Executive Governance

## Deployment

1. Replace the Apps Script project `Code.gs` with `apps-script/Code.gs` and save it.
2. Run `setupVista()` once. This adds missing columns, the `SponsorLeadership` worksheet, the `VisitResponsibilityAssignments` worksheet, and four new Config keys without replacing existing Config values.
3. To publish the six-hour EV policy as a new controlled policy version, run `publishEVChargingPolicy2026_2()` once. This deactivates the prior policy row and activates version 2026.2; it does not alter historical EV request acknowledgements.
4. Run `installSponsorLeadershipReportTrigger()` once if weekly email delivery is required. The default schedule is Monday at 06:00 in the Apps Script timezone.
5. Create a new Apps Script web-app deployment version and preserve the existing deployment URL.
6. Upload the repository frontend to GitHub Pages and hard-refresh Administration and the Sponsor Portal.

## New Config values

| Key | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SPONSOR_LEADERSHIP_EMAILS_ENABLED` | `YES` | Enables hierarchy and operational-responsibility emails. |
| `SPONSOR_LEADERSHIP_REPORTS_ENABLED` | `YES` | Enables scheduled and manual executive report dispatch. |
| `SPONSOR_LEADERSHIP_REPORT_DAY` | `MONDAY` | Weekly report day used when the trigger is installed. |
| `SPONSOR_LEADERSHIP_REPORT_HOUR` | `6` | Local Apps Script hour for weekly delivery. |

## Permission model

- Admin and Super Administrator create/end hierarchy assignments and can send leadership reports immediately.
- Sponsor and Approver users remain the primary accountable owner of their visitors.
- A primary Sponsor can assign one active Operational Sponsor to an individual visitor.
- Operational Sponsors receive scoped access to that visitor and day-of-visit arrival notifications.
- Plant Leaders see only visitors and performance belonging to Sponsors assigned to them.
- Plant Leaders do not receive Administration privileges, Security check-in/out privileges, or global audit access.

## Prove-out test

1. In Administration, open **Sponsor Approval Governance**.
2. If needed, create the leader in **VISTA Active Directory** with the new **Plant Leader** role. This role is reporting-only and does not grant visitor approval, Security, or Administration authority.
3. Select **Assign Plant Leader**, choose a Sponsor and an LL5/Plant Leader account, leave reports and notifications enabled, and save.
4. Confirm the new `SponsorLeadership` row and `SPONSOR_LEADERSHIP_ASSIGNED` Audit Log event.
5. Sign in to the Sponsor Portal as the primary Sponsor, open a visitor, and select **Assign Operational Sponsor**.
6. Confirm both the primary and operational Sponsor receive the assignment email.
7. Sign in as the operational Sponsor and confirm only the delegated visitor is visible.
8. Check that visitor in from Security Operations with Sponsor arrival notification selected. Confirm both Sponsors receive the arrival email.
9. Sign in as the Plant Leader and verify the executive Sponsor scorecard and two-week outlook contain only assigned Sponsor data. Confirm visitor decision and editing controls are not displayed.
10. From Administration select **Send Reports Now** and confirm the Plant Leader receives the branded executive snapshot email with Sponsor standings and the current/next-week visit table.
11. Move a permanent badge request through Pending Security Review, Approved for Production, In Production, Ready for Pickup, and Issued. Confirm notes, badge number, stakeholder email, Visit Activity, and Audit Log entries.

## Data retention and ownership

- `SponsorLeadership` retains hierarchy lifecycle and the administrator who created or ended it.
- `VisitResponsibilityAssignments` retains every visitor responsibility handoff, including superseded and ended assignments.
- Primary Sponsor identity on `VisitRequests` is never overwritten by operational delegation.
- Production API URLs, Drive folder IDs, email distribution values, and existing Config values are preserved.
