Security Agreement

Visitors are subject to screening and must remain within approved areas. Photography, audio/video recording, weapons, unauthorized storage devices, and removal of Ford Energy property or information are prohibited unless specifically authorized.

Visitor badges remain Ford Energy property and must be returned before departure. Visitors must follow Security instructions and remain with their escort when required.

Biometric and Facial Recognition Consent / Privacy Agreement

I understand that Ford Energy may use my submitted photograph and approved identity-verification technologies to support visitor identification, access control, safety, and security. Information will be handled according to Ford Energy privacy and retention requirements.

Safety Golden Rules

Follow energy-isolation requirements, wear assigned PPE and seatbelts, obey speed limits, do not operate equipment without authorization, report injuries and unsafe conditions, remain alert for industrial vehicles, and never bypass safety devices.

Anti-Harassment and Zero-Tolerance Policy

Bullying, harassment, threats, discrimination, retaliation, and disruptive behavior are prohibited. Visitors must treat employees, contractors, and other guests respectfully and comply with immediate direction from Ford Energy representatives.
