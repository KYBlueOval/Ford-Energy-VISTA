Agreements and Site Rules

Security Agreement

Visitors are subject to security screening and must remain within approved areas. Visitor badges remain Ford Energy property, must be visibly displayed, and must be returned before departure. Visitors must follow all Security instructions and remain with their escort whenever escorting is required.

Unauthorized access, photography, audio/video recording, removal of property or information, and attempts to bypass access-control or screening processes are prohibited.

Biometric and Facial Recognition Consent

I understand that Ford Energy may use my submitted photograph and approved identity-verification technologies, including facial comparison where authorized, to support visitor identification, access control, safety, and security.

I acknowledge that this consent applies to the visit dates listed in this request and that access may be denied or restricted when required verification cannot be completed.

Privacy Agreement

Visitor information will be collected and used for legitimate safety, security, access-control, emergency-accountability, compliance, and operational purposes. Information will be protected and retained according to Ford Energy requirements and applicable law.

I certify that the information supplied is accurate and understand that authorized personnel may review it for approval, check-in, investigation, audit, and reporting purposes.

Safety Golden Rules / SGR Compliance

Follow energy-isolation requirements, wear assigned PPE and seatbelts, obey posted speed limits, do not operate equipment without authorization, report injuries and unsafe conditions, remain alert for industrial vehicles, and never remove, bypass, or disable a safety device.

Visitors must follow emergency instructions, evacuation routes, muster procedures, and all directions issued by Ford Energy Safety, Security, or their host.

Anti-Harassment and Zero-Tolerance Agreement

Bullying, harassment, threats, discrimination, retaliation, violence, and disruptive behavior are prohibited. Visitors must treat employees, contractors, and other guests respectfully and comply with immediate direction from Ford Energy representatives.

Violations may result in removal from the property, cancellation of access, notification to the visitor’s employer, and additional action when appropriate.

Restricted Items and Electronic Device Rules

Weapons, firearms, knives, unauthorized tools, removable storage devices, external drives, and any item prohibited by posted rules are not permitted. Laptops, cameras, recording devices, USB devices, and other secured items must be declared and approved before entry.

Electronic devices may be inspected, sealed, restricted, or denied entry. Any approved security sticker or seal must remain intact until Security completes the exit process.
